Give one task 25 minutes.

What happens in a Focus Session?

It is deliberately simple: choose music, start the timer, and return to the thing you meant to do. The session timer does not stop your current music.

  1. Choose one task.Keep the goal small enough to make progress in a single session.
  2. Start 25 minutes.Open Focus Session from Home in the iPhone app; your current music can keep playing.
  3. Take five.When focus time ends, start a five-minute break, then begin another session if you want.

App timer or web player?

The free web player lets you explore Lo-Fi playlists in your browser. The 25-minute Focus Session and five-minute break timer are features of the iPhone app, not the web player. On iPhone, playback can continue in the background while you use another app or lock the screen.
