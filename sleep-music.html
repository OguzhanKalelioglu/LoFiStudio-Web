Set a timer. Rest easy.

In the iPhone app, choose when the music should stop: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, one hour, or two hours. Playback continues with the screen locked and pauses when the timer ends. The sleep timer is an iPhone feature; the free web player does not currently include it.

  1. Start a playlist.Pick a sound that feels right tonight.
  2. Open Sleep & Bedtime.Choose the sleep timer duration in the iPhone app.
  3. Put your phone down.The music keeps playing in the background until the timer ends.
